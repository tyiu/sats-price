Capabilities

Everything you need to think in sats

A focused calculator that does one job — Bitcoin, satoshis, and fiat conversion — and does it well on every device you own.

🔄

Multi‑source pricing

Live BTC/fiat rates from Coinbase and CoinGecko, refreshed with a tap — or enter your own exchange rate manually.

🧮

Instant conversion

Type into any field — Sats, BTC, or fiat — and watch every other value update immediately.

🌍

150+ currencies

Every major fiat currency with country flags, smart search, and a Priced / Unpriced split so you know what's live.

🥇

Precious metals

Track gold, silver, platinum, and palladium right alongside your fiat currencies.

📌

Personalized watchlist

Reorder currencies, pin favorites to the top, and reset with confirmation — it all persists between launches.

🔢

Locale‑aware formatting

Digit grouping and "updated" timestamps that read naturally, no matter where you are.

💻

Native on every platform

SwiftUI on iOS and macOS, Compose Multiplatform on Android — built with Kotlin Multiplatform to feel right at home on each device, not like a port.

🔓

Free & open source

GPL‑3.0 licensed, no ads, no accounts, no tracking. Inspect every line on GitHub.
